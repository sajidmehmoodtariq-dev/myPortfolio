# Storyboard timeline

All offsets refer to the same normalized GSAP timeline. The ScrollTrigger track is 230 small viewport heights on desktop and 205 on compact layouts. Chapter links target 0%, 50%, and 100%.

| Progress | Physical action | Camera |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0% | Closed cartridge with a visible face and front seam | Three-quarter view at azimuth .61 rad, elevation .53 rad |
| 15% | Assembled, with the start of the inward move | Slightly steeper orbit approaching .73 rad azimuth and .60 rad elevation |
| 30% | Cover and optic lift while lower layer separation begins | Camera eases toward the lower exploded-view angle |
| 50% | Six separated layers reveal the logic assembly and chassis aperture | Azimuth .46 rad, elevation .33 rad, nominal radius 10.65 |
| 70% | Cover has cleared the rear edge and is passing behind the stack as the other layers close | Camera pulls back and shifts its target toward the cover route |
| 85% | Assembly rotates toward vertical, the cover is sliding into its rear position, and the support begins moving down | Aim compensates for the raised cover before returning to the screen |
| 100% | Cover docked, support set, and project texture fully visible on the original optical layer | Azimuth .13 rad, elevation .062 rad, nominal radius 10.4 |

The case opens along local Y, with eased starts staggered across the six parts. The cover moves −3.21 along local Z before it descends to local Y = −.90, so it has cleared the other layers during the crossing. The shared assembly rotates a quarter turn about X, and the cover returns to local Z = 0. The base extends slightly down in the finished pose. The screen texture becomes visible from 89% through 100%.

The compact composition moves the copy above the stage, shortens the scroll track, wraps the parts list, and uses the camera's horizontal fit calculation to retain the device within the narrower frame.
