# Creative direction

**The Making of SMT** follows one object through the decisions behind Sajid Mehmood Tariq’s work. The visitor first recognizes its silhouette, then learns what each layer does and sees the parts used again in the final assembly.

| Role | Color |
| --- | --- |
| Warm cream studio | `#F5F4F0` |
| White porcelain | `#FDFDFD` |
| Etched silver | `#DBD8D0` |
| Carbon text | `#0F172A` |
| Muted steel | `#64748B` |
| Technical cyan | `#0284C7` |
| Hardware amber | `#EA580C` |

The colors above are reference tokens. The current renderer already uses related material tints and a cream studio gradient. Preserve that relationship while selecting text shades with sufficient contrast. Use cyan for interactive focus and technical markers, with amber reserved for selected hardware details.

Use satin ceramic, brushed aluminum, dark graphite, and thin frosted glass. Soft key, fill, and rim light should reveal bevels and spacing. Maintain visual continuity through the ceramic cover’s travel, a recognizable bezel, and the position of the optical surface. Leave room for each moving part, especially during the rear cover route.

Geist carries the editorial headlines and readable body text. Geist Mono identifies chapters, technical labels, and source notes. Keep essential claims and navigation in HTML. Canvas graphics illustrate the work and may have larger labels on narrow screens. Use documented captures or clearly labeled technical schematics with synthetic examples. Keep the homepage as a single scroll composition with no independent card entrances.
