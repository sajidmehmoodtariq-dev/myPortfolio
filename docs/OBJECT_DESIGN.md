# SMT Core object design

The Core is an engineered rectangular cartridge with round corner profiles, visible seams, a port, indexed vents, screws, and two small colored indicators. It occupies approximately 4.7 × 3.1 local units. The XZ plane is the mating face of each layer and Y is the stack axis. Every layer has a centered group pivot and keeps its own detail meshes.

| Part | Construction | Final role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cover | Rounded satin ceramic slab, aluminum underside, screen-printed mark | Rear cover |
| Optical interface | Thin translucent layer with a separate surface texture on its front | Project screen |
| Precision bezel | Ceramic ring with a graphite inner seat and amber index | Visible display surround |
| Logic assembly | Green board, controller, contact banks, traces, and metal packages | Internal assembly behind screen |
| Chassis | Aluminum ring with machined opening, screws, ports, and vent marks | Load-bearing enclosure |
| Base | Ceramic slab with graphite undertray and feet | Lower support edge after the display turns upright |

Rounded profiles are extruded and beveled. The bezel and chassis openings are geometric holes, so their interiors remain visible during the split. The screen texture lives on the optical layer throughout the timeline. The cover routes around the outside of the stack while the other parts return to their seats.

Warm and cool directional lights, a procedural reflection environment, and a soft projected shadow create a neutral studio. The shadow is an approximation that follows the assembly height and tilt. There is no simulated contact or physical dynamics. The material and port details are visual studies at this scale.
