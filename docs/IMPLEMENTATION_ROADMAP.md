| Milestone | Scope | Exit checks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 Foundation | Prototype checkpoint, sourced registries, five route scaffolds, metadata, and the eight-chapter plan. | Typecheck and production build. Verify the existing three scenes and direct case-study routes. |

| 05 Release review | Complete accessibility, performance, assets, and SEO checks. Confirm which routes and domain are ready for publication. | Browser and physical-device review, verified canonical and social data, and owner approval for release. |

## Open decisions and risks

- The expanded track can become tiring on touch devices. Test proposed chapter lengths and links before adding permanent copy to all eight scenes.
- Cover, optic, and bezel paths may intersect between named stops. Inspect intermediate poses and reverse each transition on desktop and mobile.
- The viewport-sized canvas needs a resolution budget that balances sharp lettering against fill cost. Compare browser frame timings and actual physical devices before release.
- The current experimental prototype starts motion regardless of browser preference at the owner’s request. Decide the public reduced-motion policy in the accessibility review. Keep all essential information available in HTML and prepare stills.
- Credential originals, approved project visuals, a portrait if wanted, public email, and publication clearance for additional work remain in intake.
- Confirm the public domain and final case-study copy before adding case pages to an index or sitemap.
